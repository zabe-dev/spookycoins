Creative rules

Use separate mobile files

Small screens need simpler copy. Do not rely on a desktop banner shrinking down.

Keep text in the green box

If the headline, ticker, logo, or CTA leaves the safe zone, it may feel cramped.

Stay lightweight

PNG, JPG, GIF, or WEBP is fine. Aim for 150–200KB per image when possible.
